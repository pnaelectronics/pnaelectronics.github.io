INKWELL

MYSTERIES

Every word hides a clue.

Inkwell Mysteries is a detective word game. Each case gives you a crime, four suspects and six crime scenes. At every scene, an evidence photo hides the answers to a picture crossword. Work out what the pictures are telling you, crack the hidden clue word, and name the culprit.

Features

  • 20 detective cases in five File Boxes
  • Picture crosswords, from simple deductions to tricky double meanings
  • 300 bonus Cold Files for endless puzzling
  • Hints, daily gifts and free lives every day
  • Plays offline
